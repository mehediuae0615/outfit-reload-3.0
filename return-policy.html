CUSTOMER CARE

Return Policy

Our return information and how to contact us if an order does not meet expectations.

7-day return policy

Our product-shopping pages state that a 7-day return policy applies. If you need to request a return, contact customer service within 7 days of receiving your order and provide your order number and the phone number used at checkout.

How to request a return

  1. Contact customer service at +880 1404-609229 within 7 days of receiving the order.
  2. Share your order number and explain the reason for the request.
  3. Our team will review the request and provide the next steps.

Return approval and refunds

Return eligibility, product condition requirements, collection or shipping arrangements and any applicable refund method must be confirmed by customer service for the specific order before sending an item back. Please do not dispatch a return until our team has provided instructions. This page does not promise an automatic refund or a particular refund-processing time.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, contact us promptly with your order details and a clear description of the issue so we can review it.

Contact

For all return enquiries, use our Contact Us page or call +880 1404-609229.
