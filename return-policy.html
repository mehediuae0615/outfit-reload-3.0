Return & Refund Policy.

Your satisfaction matters to us. Please review our return and exchange guidelines.

1. Return Window

Customers may request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving their order. Please contact us within this period so we can review your request.

2. Eligible Return Conditions

A product may be eligible for return or exchange if:

  • You received a damaged or defective product.
  • You received an incorrect item, size or colour compared with your confirmed order.
  • The product has a verified quality issue.

All requests are subject to review and approval by Outfit Reload 3.0.

3. Product Condition

For a return or exchange, the item should generally be unused, unwashed and in its original condition, with tags and packaging where applicable.

Products damaged through misuse, washing, alteration or normal wear may not qualify for a return unless applicable consumer law requires otherwise.

4. Non-Returnable Situations

Unless the product is defective, incorrect or otherwise covered by applicable law, we may decline returns for:

  • Items used, washed or altered after delivery.
  • Items damaged due to improper handling by the customer.
  • Requests made after the 7-day return period.

5. Exchange Policy

Exchange requests for a different size or colour may be considered depending on stock availability. Please contact us before sending any product back. An exchange is not confirmed until approved by our team.

6. Refund Policy

If a return is approved and a refund is appropriate, we will contact you to confirm the refund arrangement.

For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund method and timing will be agreed with the customer. Refunds are subject to verification of the returned item.

7. Return Shipping

If the return is due to an incorrect, defective or damaged item supplied by us, we will discuss an appropriate return delivery arrangement with you.

For other approved requests, any return delivery charge will be communicated before the return is arranged.

8. How to Request a Return

To submit a return or exchange request:

  • Contact us through WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving the product.
  • Provide your order number and contact phone number.
  • Explain the reason for your request.
  • Share clear photographs or videos if the item is damaged or incorrect.
  • Wait for our approval and return instructions before sending the item.

Need help with a return?
Contact our support team with your order number.

9. Policy Updates

Outfit Reload 3.0 may update this policy when necessary. The latest version will be available on this page. Nothing in this policy limits any rights customers have under applicable consumer protection laws in Bangladesh.
