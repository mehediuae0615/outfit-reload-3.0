Shipping Policy.

Everything you need to know about order delivery and shipping.

These are estimated timeframes, not guaranteed delivery dates. Remote locations, courier availability, weather and other unexpected circumstances may cause delays.

4. Order Processing

After receiving your order, we may contact you to verify your details and confirm availability. Orders are prepared for dispatch after confirmation.

5. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for eligible delivery locations. Customers should pay the confirmed order amount to the delivery person when receiving the parcel.

6. Delivery Address and Contact Details

Please provide an accurate delivery address and an active phone number during checkout. Incorrect or incomplete details may result in delivery delays or failed delivery attempts.

7. Failed Delivery Attempts

If the courier cannot deliver your parcel, they may contact you to arrange another attempt. Additional delivery arrangements or charges may apply depending on the circumstances.

8. Delivery Delays

Although we aim to meet our estimated delivery times, delays may occur due to public holidays, severe weather, transportation issues or other circumstances beyond our control.
