Privacy Policy.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Outfit Reload 3.0. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or place an order with us.

By using our website, you acknowledge this policy. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

2. Information We Collect

When you browse our website or place an order, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name.
  • Phone number and contact details.
  • Delivery address and location details.
  • Order details, purchased products and delivery preferences.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting our support team.

We may also receive basic technical information, such as browser type, device information and website usage data, where available.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information for legitimate store operations, including:

  • Processing and confirming orders.
  • Arranging product delivery.
  • Contacting customers about their orders.
  • Providing customer support and handling returns or complaints.
  • Improving our website, products and services.
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activities.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customers' personal information.

We may share necessary information with delivery partners, website hosting providers and technology services when required to operate the store, process orders or provide customer support.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful request from an authorized authority.

5. Payments

Our store currently offers Cash on Delivery (COD). We do not ask customers to share bank passwords, card PINs or one-time verification codes through our website or customer support channels.

If additional payment methods are introduced in the future, their relevant payment providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences and understand website performance.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online platform or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, business records, dispute resolution and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information that we hold about you.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting or legitimate business purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, such as social media platforms and delivery services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their respective privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general shopping customers and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
