Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse, create an account or place an order with Outfit Reload 3.0.

1. Information we collect

When you use Outfit Reload 3.0, we may collect information you provide during shopping or account use, including your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account details, order details, product reviews and information you submit when contacting us.

When you browse the site, basic technical information may also be processed by the hosting platform and services needed to operate the website.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain customer accounts.
  • To process orders, arrange delivery and provide order updates.
  • To verify order enquiries and bKash delivery-payment details where that checkout option is enabled.
  • To respond to customer questions and manage product reviews.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the website and prevent misuse.

3. Service providers and data storage

Outfit Reload 3.0 uses Supabase services for parts of its account, database and website functionality. Information needed for these functions may be processed and stored by that provider on our behalf. We do not publish customer passwords or payment PINs.

4. Cookies, advertising and third parties

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember shopping-related information and improve site functionality. If third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads as permitted by their policies and applicable law. Visitors can manage advertising personalisation through Google's ad settings. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

We use information when needed to operate the store, process and deliver orders, provide customer support, maintain the website or comply with legal obligations. We do not intentionally make customer account or order details publicly available. We do not ask customers to share account passwords, bKash PINs or one-time codes with us.

6. Data retention and your choices

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for account services, order records, customer support and legitimate business or legal purposes. You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account or to request correction or deletion where applicable. Some order or transaction records may need to be retained.

7. Children's privacy

Our website is a general fashion-shopping service and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions, contact +880 1404-609229 or use our Contact Us page.
